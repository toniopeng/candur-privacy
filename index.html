Candur

Privacy Policy · Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Candur ("we", "our", or "the app") is a daily self-reflection app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Candur app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Candur is distributed through the Apple App Store.

In short: we do not track you, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

◆

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide the service. In App Store "App Privacy" terms:

  • Contact Info (Name, Email Address): Candur uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us your name and email address only if you choose to share them. If you choose "Hide My Email", we receive an Apple private relay address instead of your real email. We use this to create and identify your account.
  • User Content (your reflections): The text you write each day, the mood you select, and the reflection response you receive are stored in your account so you can see them in your archive.
  • Identifiers (User ID): A unique account ID created by our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication) when you sign in with Apple. It links your reflections to your account.

All of the above is linked to your account and used only to provide app functionality. None of it is used for tracking or advertising.

We do not collect your precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, or advertising identifier (IDFA).

◆

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account (Sign in with Apple)
  • To generate your daily AI reflection response
  • To store and display your reflection archive
  • To unlock Candur+ features you have purchased
  • To send your daily reminder notification, if you turn it on
◆

4. AI Processing (DeepSeek)

To write your reflection response, the text you submit is sent from the app to our own server (a Firebase Cloud Function), which forwards it to the DeepSeek large language model API and returns the response to you. Only the text of your entry is sent for this purpose; we do not send your name, email address, or account ID to DeepSeek. Your app never talks to DeepSeek directly, and our API credentials are kept on our server, not in the app.

Use of the DeepSeek API is subject to DeepSeek's own terms and privacy policy. We do not use your reflections to train AI models, and we do not sell your reflections. Please avoid including information in your reflections that you would not want processed in this way.

◆

5. No Tracking, No Ads

Candur does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not contain third-party advertising, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not share data with data brokers.

◆

6. Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is the only way to sign in on iPhone. Apple's handling of your Apple ID is described in Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/legal/privacy. When you delete your Candur account, we also revoke Candur's Sign in with Apple authorization where Apple allows it. You can also review or stop using Sign in with Apple for Candur at any time in Settings › [your name] › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple.

◆

7. Purchases and Subscriptions (Apple In-App Purchase)

Candur+ is offered as an in-app purchase through the Apple App Store. Payment is processed entirely by Apple using your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment card or billing details. Apple may share purchase status with us (for example, whether Candur+ is active) so we can unlock features. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you can manage or cancel them in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or from Candur's Settings screen. Refunds are handled by Apple.

◆

8. Data Storage, Service Providers and Retention

  • Your account and reflections are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as our service provider and processes data on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • The AI reflection is generated via our server and the DeepSeek API, as described in Section 4.
  • Free accounts can view their 7 most recent reflections; older reflections are retained but require Candur+ to view.
  • Your reminder time is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • We keep your data until you delete your account.

We do not sell your personal data.

◆

9. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and data at any time inside the app: Settings › Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm with Sign in with Apple. Deleting your account removes your reflections, mood records, subscription records and profile from our systems and deletes your sign-in account. This cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID subscription settings. If you cannot access the app, email us at taopeng46@gmail.com and we will delete your account. See also Account & Data Deletion.

◆

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can delete your data in the app at any time. To exercise any other right, contact us at taopeng46@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

◆

11. Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and access to your reflections is restricted to your account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

◆

12. Children's Privacy

Candur is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

◆

13. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Candur asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. Your chosen time stays on your device.

◆

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify you in the app.
