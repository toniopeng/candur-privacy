Privacy Policy · Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Candur ("we", "our", or "the app") is a daily self-reflection app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Candur app handles, how we use it, and the choices you have. Candur is distributed through the Apple App Store.

In short: Candur has no accounts and no sign-in. Your reflections are stored only on your device. We do not track you, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

◆

2. Information We Handle

  • No account, no sign-in: You do not create an account and you do not give us your name, email address, or any other contact information. We do not assign you a user ID.
  • Your reflections (stored on your device only): The text you write each day, the mood you select, and the reflection response you receive are saved only on your iPhone. We do not keep a copy on any server.
  • The text of each reflection (sent for processing): When you ask for a reflection, the text you wrote is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, a Cloudflare Worker, which forwards it to DeepSeek and returns the response to you. See Section 4.
  • Rate limiting: To prevent abuse, the app sends a random, anonymous identifier generated on your device when it requests a reflection. It is not linked to you, your Apple ID, or your reflections, and it is used only to count daily requests.

We do not collect your precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, or advertising identifier (IDFA).

◆

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate your daily AI reflection response
  • To limit the number of requests per device to keep the service available
  • To unlock Candur+ features you have purchased through Apple
  • To send your daily reminder notification, if you turn it on (scheduled locally on your device)
◆

4. AI Processing (Cloudflare Worker and DeepSeek)

To write your reflection response, the text of your entry is sent from the app to our own proxy server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to the DeepSeek large language model API and returns the response to you. Only the text of your entry is sent for this purpose. It is sent without your name, email address, or any account or advertising identifier, and our proxy does not store the text of your reflections. Your app never talks to DeepSeek directly, and our API credentials are kept on our server, not in the app.

Use of the DeepSeek API is subject to DeepSeek's own terms and privacy policy. We do not use your reflections to train AI models, and we do not sell your reflections. Please avoid including information in your reflections that you would not want processed in this way.

◆

5. No Tracking, No Ads

Candur does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not contain third-party advertising, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not share data with data brokers.

◆

6. Purchases and Subscriptions (Apple In-App Purchase)

Candur+ is offered as an in-app purchase through the Apple App Store. Payment is processed entirely by Apple using your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment card or billing details. Candur checks your purchase status directly with Apple on your device to unlock features. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you can manage or cancel them in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or from the Candur+ screen. You can restore purchases on a new device from the Candur+ screen. Refunds are handled by Apple.

◆

7. Data Storage and Retention

  • Your reflections, moods, and reminder time are stored only on your device. They stay there until you erase them or delete the app.
  • Free users can view their 7 most recent reflections; Candur+ unlocks your full history on your device.
  • Our proxy server processes each reflection request to return a response and does not keep the text of your reflections. It keeps only anonymous daily request counters.
  • Because your reflections are not stored on our servers, we cannot recover them if you delete the app or lose your device.

We do not sell your personal data.

◆

8. Deleting Your Data

Candur has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. To erase your data, use Erase my data at the bottom of the Archive screen, or delete the Candur app from your iPhone. Either removes all reflections stored on your device. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID subscription settings. See also Data Deletion.

◆

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights regarding personal data. Because we do not hold your name, email, or reflections, we generally have no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our side; you control your reflections directly on your device. If you have any request or question, contact us at taopeng46@gmail.com.

◆

10. Data Security

Reflection text is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections, and your reflections are kept in your device's app storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

◆

11. Children's Privacy

Candur is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

◆

12. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Candur asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. Your chosen time stays on your device.
