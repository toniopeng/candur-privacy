Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Account

You can delete your Candur account and all associated data directly within the app.

How to Delete In-App

  • Open the Candur app
  • Tap the Settings icon (top right)
  • Scroll to the bottom
  • Tap Delete Account
  • Confirm deletion
◆

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All reflection entries and AI responses
  • Mood and emotional tone records
  • Subscription status and history
  • All personal data associated with your account
◆

Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at:

taopeng46@gmail.com

◆

Data Retention

Upon deletion, all your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.
